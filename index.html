help Help

calculate Calculator

Tap number buttons to type. Tap operator buttons (+, -, x, /) to build an equation. Tap = to see the answer. You can use your phone's keyboard too (go to Settings to enable it).

Switch between Basic and Scientific mode using the toggle above the keypad. Scientific mode adds sin, cos, tan, square root, powers, logarithms, constants, and more.

timer Stopwatch & Timer

Stopwatch: Tap Start to begin. While running, you can Stop (pause), Record Lap, or tap Reset when paused to zero it out.

Timer: Set hours, minutes, and seconds using the input fields, then tap Start. While running you can Pause. When paused, Resume or Reset. You can change the time any time by editing the inputs and tapping Start again.

alarm Alarms

Set the hour and minute, optionally add a label, choose a repeat option (Once, Daily, Weekdays), pick a ringtone, and tap Add. Alarms trigger when your device's time matches.

Custom Ringtones: Tap the upload button to add your own audio files. They are stored locally on your device.

Snooze: When an alarm rings, tap Snooze to delay it by the duration set in Settings.

swap_horiz Converters & Tools

Unit Converter: Choose a category (Length, Weight, Temperature, etc.), then select the "from" and "to" units. Type a number to see the conversion instantly.

Currency Converter: Shows live exchange rates for 70+ currencies. Pick two currencies and type an amount. Rates update automatically.

Percentage Tools: 7 built-in calculators: What is X% of Y, Discount, Tax, Tip, Margin, Markup, and Percentage Change.

Date & Time: Find the difference between dates, add/subtract days, calculate age, count business days, and see Unix timestamps.

Weather: Auto-detects your location or search any city. Shows temperature, humidity, wind, UV index, sunrise/sunset, and a 3-day forecast.

schedule World Clock

Add any timezone to see the current time there. Tap the + button to search cities. Tap the star to save favorites. Toggle between 12h/24h format from the quick settings.

history History

Every calculation is saved. Tap the history icon in the top-right to open the history panel. Tap any entry to recall it to the calculator. You can copy or delete individual entries, or clear all history at once.

palette Themes & Appearance

Tap the gear icon for Settings, or the palette icon for Themes. Choose from 12 built-in themes (Dark, Light, Aurora, Ocean, Emerald, Sunset, Violet, Rose, Cyber, Arctic, Solar, Forest).

Quick Settings: The gear icon in the header also has a quick settings panel for fast access to theme, accent color, dark/light mode, clock format, and angle unit.

backup Backup & Restore

Go to Profile to Export all your data (settings, history, preferences) as a JSON file. You can import it later or on another device to restore everything.
